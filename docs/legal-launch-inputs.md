# Saudi legal launch inputs

Status: **draft checklist only — not legal advice**.

The site owner confirmed that they have a Saudi freelance-work document. The official Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development describes this document as a service for freelance practitioners. It does not, by itself, provide the website with the missing identity, contact, privacy, or activity-specific legal text.

Before an unprotected public launch, collect and review the following with a qualified Saudi legal adviser where appropriate:

1. **Controller / provider identity**: the exact full name from the valid freelance-work document. Do not publish the national ID, document number, or document image.
2. **Public service-contact channel**: a dedicated business email or business phone/WhatsApp number. The current personal number must not be silently used as the privacy-contact channel.
3. **Activity match**: confirm that the actual activity on the freelance-work document covers how the club is operated. This is an owner/legal review, not an engineering inference.
4. **Privacy request channel and workflow**: who receives access, correction, deletion, and consent-withdrawal requests; how identity will be checked; and the response process.
5. **Final venue and cancellation wording**: the approved venue identity/address and any event-specific cancellation or change policy that is not already implemented.
6. **Publication review**: replace the visible draft notices in `/privacy` and `/terms`, add an effective date, and perform a final Saudi legal review before removing Vercel Authentication.

- Ministry of Commerce: [E-commerce law portal](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/ECC/pages/default.aspx)

The SDAIA guideline identifies the policy content that still needs owner-provided facts: data collected, purpose, legal basis, sharing parties, geographic processing scope, retention/deletion, data-subject rights, and contact method. The application already has evidence for collection categories and retention; it must not invent the remaining facts.
