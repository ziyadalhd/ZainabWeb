# Saudi legal launch inputs

Status: **draft checklist only — not legal advice**.

The site owner confirmed that they have a Saudi freelance-work document. The official Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development describes this document as a service for freelance practitioners. It does not, by itself, provide the website with the missing identity, contact, privacy, or activity-specific legal text.

Before an unprotected public launch, collect and review the following with a qualified Saudi legal adviser where appropriate:

1. **Controller / provider identity**: the owner does not approve publishing their personal legal name. Confirm whether the club's distinctive name plus an appropriate public verification record satisfies the applicable disclosure requirement, or whether another registered operating form is required. Do not publish the national ID or a document image.
2. **Public service-contact channel**: `bayn.collective@gmail.com` is approved as the public privacy and customer-contact email. The existing public phone remains the current operational phone.
3. **Activity match**: the owner supplied the current document activity privately. It does not clearly establish coverage for every service offered through the site, so a qualified review or an appropriately matching activity is still required. Do not record private document details in Git.
4. **Privacy request channel and workflow**: who receives access, correction, deletion, and consent-withdrawal requests; how identity will be checked; and the response process.
5. **Final venue and cancellation wording**: Makkah is confirmed as the city. The approved venue identity/full address and any event-specific cancellation or change policy that is not already implemented are still required.
6. **Publication review**: replace the visible draft notices in `/privacy` and `/terms`, add an effective date, and perform a final Saudi legal review before removing Vercel Authentication.
